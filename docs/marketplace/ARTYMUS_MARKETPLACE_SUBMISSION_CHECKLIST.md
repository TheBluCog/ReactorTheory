## Submission Positioning

ARTYMUS 3.0 should be submitted as:

Governed agentic artificial intelligence infrastructure.

Not a chatbot.

Not a model wrapper.

Not a dashboard.

A trust and execution governance layer for agentic systems.

## Core Marketplace Claim

ARTYMUS makes agentic artificial intelligence callable, governable, auditable, and contract-bound.

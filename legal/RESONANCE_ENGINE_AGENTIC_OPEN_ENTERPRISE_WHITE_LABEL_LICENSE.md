This Resonance Engine Agentic Open Enterprise White Label License Contract establishes the terms under which an approved Licensee may white-label, integrate, operate, or distribute the **Resonance Engine** and related Reactor Theory / RT9 / RT11 governance logic inside enterprise software, agentic artificial intelligence systems, workflow automation, decision-support platforms, dashboards, private clouds, or commercial large language model products.

A **White Label Deployment** means any deployment where the Licensee markets, presents, embeds, rebrands, resells, or operates the Resonance Engine or any derivative functionality under the Licensee's own brand, client brand, partner brand, or private enterprise label.

**Enterprise Use** means use by or for any company, government entity, non-profit institution, platform, regulated business, investment vehicle, professional services firm, software vendor, systems integrator, or commercial user.

**Open Enterprise License** means the commercial posture of this agreement: broad enterprise implementation is permitted only under the attribution, safety, governance, audit, and fee obligations stated here.

A **Derivative Work** means any modification, adaptation, translation, training data transformation, embedding, model fine-tune, prompt system, fork, API, plugin, dashboard, documentation set, or workflow based on or materially inspired by the Resonance Engine.
